## Steps only an author can finish

1. Open https://aiei2027.org/submission.html and create the author account with the email above. The page sends a verification code to that inbox.
2. Choose Full paper and the track above.
3. Paste the title, abstract, and author block from `article/PORTAL.txt`.
4. Upload `article/manuscript.pdf`.
5. Download the publication-right form from the link in the table, sign it, and upload the signed PDF. The signature is not in this repository.
6. Confirm the submission and keep the receipt.

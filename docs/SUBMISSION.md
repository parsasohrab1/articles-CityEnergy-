## Steps only an author can finish

1. Open https://aiei2027.org/submission.html and create the author account with the email above.
2. Choose AIEI 2027, as the page instructs (it points authors to EasyChair).
3. Paste the title, abstract, and author block into the form.
4. Upload `article/manuscript.pdf`.
5. Download the publication-right form from that same portal, sign it, and upload the signed PDF. The signature is not in this repository.
6. Confirm the submission and keep the receipt.

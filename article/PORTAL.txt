Title: Previous-year hourly electricity forecasts the next year

Abstract:
We forecast hourly electricity in the next calendar year from the same building's previous year. On site Rat of the Building Data Genome Project 2, the hour-of-week mean is fit only on hours before 2017-01-01T00:00:00. 8 other buildings set a split-conformal interval and 8 buildings are scored on the later year. The interval covered 65865/69506 test-year hours (0.9476) at a nominal coverage of 0.9000. Its mean width was 49.8700 kWh. The mean test-year load was 36.2297 kWh and the mean absolute error was 9.9583 kWh. A profile transferred from other buildings, fit without the target building's own year, had mean absolute error 14.7358 kWh and mean width 151.8214 kWh on the same hours.

Keywords: building electricity; next-year forecast; prediction interval; Building Data Genome 2
